Cultural Spaces

Music is deeply connected to culture, it is a way to express tradition, beliefs, and identities. Across the world, communities use music to celebrate important events, preserve stories and pass traditions through generations. Whether music is performed during traditional festivals, religious ceremonies, family gatherings or national celebrations, music creates spaces where people are able to create exposure for their heritage and connect with other communities. These spaces remind people to preserve their identities and an important part of cultural expression.
