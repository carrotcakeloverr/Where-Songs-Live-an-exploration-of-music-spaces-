Physical Spaces

From vinyls, CD’s and record stores, there are several formats that transcend the subjective use of soundwaves into a connection within the physical spaces where it is performed and experienced. Starting with the comfort of a song in a childhood bedroom, walking through the streets, or going to a live concert.

The environment heavily influences the way that people discover and engage with music. The personal small nature of a room, the size of a venue or a place with several people gathered shapes a listening experience.

With the creation of community through music, people attending festivals, concerts, or theaters, it unites them using live sound. In more personal spaces, music becomes a tool for creativity, relaxation, and mediation. Physical spaces are the manifestation of the impact that music has on individuals that can be remembered.
