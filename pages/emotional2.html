Music and Memory

With a powerful connection to memory, music can instantly remind a person of a specific moment, place, or person. Lowering risks of dementia by 40%, it helps protect cognition, and it is a link between music and mental and emotional well-being, it can help the brain remember significant events and recollect events that already happened. Music also impacts the ability for the brain to focus, depending on the type of music that is consumed, it can improve concentration. Music with a slow tempo, lacking lyrics and ambient sounds can promote the state of calmness, minimizing distraction. Because of the benefits that implement music into everyday life, it allows a connection that enhances lasting reminders of meaningful moments.

Music and Well-being

As an integral part of human culture and practices, music can be used to address stress-related illnesses and various mental disorders such as anxiety or depression. It provides a safe environment to heal from trauma and building relicense while decreasing anxiety levels. Listening to sad music can influence the listener’s mood to feel unpleasant emotions, while listening to happy beats empowers a more energetic or happy mood. The feeling of belonging that is felt from music is also crucial for mental health, surrounded by people that share the same interests improve quality of life and community engagement.

Music and Identity

Reflecting personal and collective experiences, it is a core part of an individual’s identity. Streaming platforms and social media enabled people to discover, share and produce songs that are accessible throughout the world, making communities that align with the exploration of gender identity, personal values, interests, and cultural backgrounds. As people grow and develop, their music taste matures as well, as different playlists represent a stage on their personal life. Music becomes more than entertainment for people, but a way to understand themselves, express who they are and share their identity with others.

Before physical concerts take place, music begins with a deep curiosity inside of a home. Bedrooms, living rooms and home studios are places where people are able to create new music from people around them through speakers or simply enjoy artists inside of headphones. Many music artists are modern in a way that it is not required to record music with big budgets, but instead prefer a more personal approach by producing quality music at home. Musical spaces can appear in many ways that allow creativity in different kinds of places, whether it is small or large expensive productions.

Music is generally part of a person's everyday life, heard in coffee shops, shopping centers, airports and even restaurants, it gives the places that people inhabit a new perspective and unique mood. Public spaces allow individuals to be exposed to different cultures, new sounds and even a place to share the same feelings with an audience. Whether it is street musicians performing in sidewalks, hearing a popular song through the park or background music in an elevator, these everyday environments show how music becomes part of the places of everyday and how it moves with people.
