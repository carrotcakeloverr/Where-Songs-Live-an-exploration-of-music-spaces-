Digital Spaces

Digital spaces were the first clear transition that shaped and transformed how people experience music. Instead of having the limitation of having vinyl records that only play one full album at a time, physical locations like concert halls or a Walkman that needs to be manually customized to the users liking, digital music makes accessibility seem like a second nature to the listener.

Modern music exists within streaming platforms, social media, online communities, and virtual events. Because of the interconnected purpose of music now, people are able to share playlists, discover new artists, and even attend performances across the world without leaving the comfort of their own home.

As technology continues to evolve, digital spaces have become one of the most influential places where music is created and distributed.
